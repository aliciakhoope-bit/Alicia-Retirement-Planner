Alicia Retirement Planner

A client-facing interactive retirement planning tool by Alicia.

Features

* Singapore retirement gap illustration
* CPF accumulation projection
* Private investment projection
* Retirement income target calculation
* Inflation and investment return assumptions
* Lump-sum amount required today
* Monthly savings required
* Cost of Waiting — shows how delaying retirement planning can increase the required monthly savings
* 1–10 year delay comparison
* Mobile and iPad-friendly interface

Disclaimer

This tool is for illustration and discussion purposes only. The calculations use assumptions and simplified projections and should not be treated as a guarantee of future investment returns, CPF balances, or retirement outcomes.

Actual CPF rules, contribution rates, interest rates, product terms and individual circumstances may differ. Please refer to the relevant official sources and policy documents for actual figures and benefits.
